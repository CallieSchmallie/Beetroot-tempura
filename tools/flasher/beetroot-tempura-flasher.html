Beetroot Tempura flasher

Installs the firmware on an M-VAVE FM-1 over USB, using M-VAVE's own FM1 Open update code.

1 Choose the firmware

Select both files from your build folder build\dx7\: generated\FM1_OFFICIAL_OPEN.fwsc and build-identity.json.

2 Connect the FM-1

Plug the FM-1 into this computer with a USB data cable and switch it on. Close other MIDI apps and any other workbench tabs.

3 Before you flash

4 Flash

If anything goes wrong, leave the FM-1 connected and press Install again: the device stays in its update loader until a complete image is written. You can always return to FM1 Open or stock V15 from the M-VAVE workbench at open.m-vave.com.

5 Load sounds

Beetroot Tempura makes grains from the FM-1's eight sample slots. If the tracks on the FM-1 show a dash, the slots are empty and it stays silent. Load eight demo textures (choir, kalimba, strings, glass, arpeggio, breath, drone, vox), or your own WAVs with M-VAVE's workbench sample manager.

This replaces the FM-1's whole sample bank. Back it up first if it has sounds you want to keep.
